# Applied Quantitative Methods

Notebooks and data for Applied Quantitative Methods (AQM), 3rd semester HA and EBA, Aalborg University Business School, Fall 2026. Slides, messages and other course information are on Moodle.

All notebooks open in Google Colab from the links below. To keep your own changes, save a copy to your Google Drive (File, Save a copy in Drive).

The lecture notebook holds the exercises we do during the lecture and all calculations from the slides. In the workshop notebook you write the code yourselves. From session 3, a book exercises notebook gives extra exercises in the style of the textbook. Solutions to the workshop and the book exercises are added to the session's folder after the session.

## Exam assignment

The exam assignment is in [exam_assignment.ipynb](https://colab.research.google.com/github/aaubs/AQM-2026/blob/main/exam/exam_assignment.ipynb). Save a copy in your Google Drive before you start. Each group hands in the completed notebook in Digital Eksamen by 13 January 2027.

## Data

The data are in the `data` folder and are described in [data/README.md](data/README.md): simulated data from RetailCorp, a fictional online retailer, used from Lecture 1, a public airline passenger survey used from Lecture 3, and the data for the exam assignment. The notebooks load them directly from this repository, so you do not need to download anything.
